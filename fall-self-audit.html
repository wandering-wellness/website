Set the Direction

Now turn toward what's ahead. Fall rewards a short list, not a long one.

Your top priorities

If you could only make real progress on two or three things this season, what would they be? What deserves your focus, and what can honestly wait?

One word for the season

If this fall went exactly the way you needed it to, how would it feel? Not a goal — a feeling. Find the one word.

Mornings & evenings

The edges of your day set the tone for the middle. How do you want your mornings to feel? Your evenings?
